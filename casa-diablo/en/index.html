The house

Double height inside, almost 1,000 m² outside.

Behind the front, the house opens into a double-height living room with a wood and wrought-iron staircase, an interior balcony and arched windows. Around it, almost 1,000 m² of mostly flat land, with a back patio, terrace and enclosed garage.

Upstairs are the three bedrooms, each with its own bathroom. The main bedroom has a study area, walk-in closet and jetted tub. Downstairs: living room, dining room, den, kitchen with granite countertops, guest half bath, laundry and maid's room with bathroom.

The house is currently used as an office: the two secondary bedrooms are joined into one room and the ground floor has work areas. Finishes are from the 2008 renovation.

Questions

What people ask me most.

How big is it?

According to the October 2025 appraisal by Panamericana de Avalúos: a 994.97 m² registered lot and 321.97 m² built, on two floors.

How many bedrooms and bathrooms?

Three bedrooms, each with its own bathroom, plus a guest half bath downstairs and a maid's room with bathroom. Two of the bedrooms are currently joined into one room used as an office.

Why does it look like an office?

It is currently used as an office by a company. The original layout is residential and can go back to that use. The handover date is confirmed with the owner before an offer.

Is it sold furnished?

The current office furniture can be included; it is discussed in the offer. The price does not include appliances, air conditioners or light fixtures.

What is the appraised value?

The October 2025 appraisal by Panamericana de Avalúos set the market value at US$625,000. The asking price is US$550,000.

Are the photos edited?

They are real photographs of the house, adjusted for light and colour. Two photos were cropped on one side to leave out the occupying company's branding, and the house number was hidden on the front views.

How do I arrange a visit?

By appointment. Tell me which day works for you, whether you're buying in cash or with financing, and your timeline.

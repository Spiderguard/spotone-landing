Let’s talk

I’m an industrial engineer, and I’ve been a real estate broker since 2006.

I work on sales, purchases and rentals of property in Panama.

My specialty is Panama City: Costa del Este, Costa Sur, San Francisco, Punta Pacífica and other areas of the city. There I track what’s listed, at what asking price and for how long.

See the areas →

Outside those areas, and for commercial spaces, land, farms, beach properties, pre-construction or bank-owned properties, I study each case with the same method.

I work with other brokers, so your property reaches more buyers. And if another broker has the listing you want, I coordinate with them so you can see it.

I’m your contact from start to finish. And if the best decision is not to go ahead, I’ll tell you.

Outside work, I train Brazilian jiu-jitsu, and I’m a music lover.

Message me on WhatsApp: what you have or what you are looking for, in which area and by when.

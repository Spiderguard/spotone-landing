Relocation

Moving to Panama.

Where you live depends on your work, daily journeys and family needs. That is where we start your property search.

My work here is helping you choose the area and the home, compare options and arrange the viewings. Legal, immigration and school matters stay with the specialists who handle them.

01 · The process

Area comparison

We assess neighborhoods based on your regular journeys, nearby services and the type of home you want.

Family and routines

I review how the daily distances work out from each area: schools, work and the journeys you will make every week.

A single point of contact

I coordinate the search and viewings with you, including when other brokers are involved.

Remote process available

We can start your property search before you arrive in Panama and arrange viewings for your arrival.

02 · Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How does a foreigner buy property in Panama?

In most cases a foreigner can acquire property in Panama. The purchase structure, any restrictions that may apply and the documentation should be reviewed with a lawyer before signing or transferring funds. I walk you through the process and coordinate with your lawyer; this information is for guidance and does not replace legal review.

Can we start before I move to Panama?

Yes. The process can begin remotely: we compare areas, review prices and evaluate options over WhatsApp or video call. You do not need to be in the country to make decisions with real information.

03 · Let’s talk

When do you arrive in Panama?

With your date and the size of your family I build the calendar: area, viewings, lease and move, in that order.

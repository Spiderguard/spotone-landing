Relocation

Moving to Panama.

Whether you rent first or buy from day one, the right home depends on where you’ll work, where your children will go to school and how long you’ll spend in traffic. That’s where we start.

I help you choose the area and the home, compare options and coordinate the viewings. Legal, immigration and school matters stay with the specialists who handle them, and I coordinate with your lawyer.

The process

Choosing an area

We compare neighborhoods by real rush-hour times to your work and your children’s school, nearby services and the kind of home you want. Not by “close to.”

More in Criterio: Where to live in Panama

Renting

Before you sign, I confirm in writing what the rent includes (condo fee, appliances, furniture) and whether that building allows pets. Utilities such as electricity, gas and internet are stated in the listing.

More in Criterio: What “línea blanca” includes in a rental

Buying

In most cases a foreigner can buy property in Panama; the purchase structure is reviewed with your lawyer before you sign. I check the title and any mortgage or lien at the Public Registry, and deposits go to a lawyer’s account or a trust, never in cash.

More in Criterio: Buying from abroad · Titled property vs. rights of possession · Mortgage requirements

Banking, residency and schools

Bank accounts, immigration and school enrollment are handled by the specialists who do them. I keep the property search in step with those steps, so the lease or purchase is ready when you need it.

Starting from abroad

We can start before you arrive: video viewings, and signing through a power of attorney when needed. In-person viewings are lined up for your arrival.

One point of contact

In Panama a property often passes through several brokers. I coordinate with them; you deal only with me, start to finish.

Questions.

How does a foreigner buy property in Panama?

In most cases a foreigner can acquire property in Panama. The purchase structure, any restrictions that may apply and the documentation should be reviewed with a lawyer before signing or transferring funds. I walk you through the process and coordinate with your lawyer; this information is for guidance and does not replace legal review.

Can we start before I move to Panama?

Yes. The process can begin remotely: we compare areas, review prices and evaluate options over WhatsApp or video call. You do not need to be in the country to make decisions with real information.

Do you only work with the properties you represent?

No. Besides what I represent, I work with properties from other brokers, banks and developers, and I coordinate with building management. Legal, immigration and school matters are handled by each specialist. You deal only with me, from start to finish.

Are pets allowed?

It depends on the owner and the building's rules. For each property we confirm it in writing before the viewing.

Is the maintenance fee included in the rent?

In residential rentals it usually is. Whatever isn't included (electricity, gas, internet) is stated in the listing.

What does "furnished" include, and what does "línea blanca" include?

Furnished = furniture; línea blanca = appliances. We put the details in writing.

Buying and investing

Does it have a mortgage or any lien?

We check it in the Public Registry before you sign anything.

How much is the maintenance fee per m² in this building?

We give you the building's exact amount and what it covers.

More in Criterio: A low maintenance fee isn't always good news.

How much does an apartment here rent for, net?

We calculate net rent with maintenance, vacancy and taxes.

More in Criterio: Buy to rent? · Before I make an offer, there are three numbers I almost always check first.

What does your commission include? Who pays it?

In a sale the seller pays it, at closing. It covers pricing, presentation, buyer screening, negotiation and support through registration.

How do I verify the agent and the seller?

A PN license you can check with the JTBR, the title in the Public Registry, and funds only to a lawyer's or escrow account.

More in Criterio: Would your agent buy it?

How is the deposit or reservation money handled?

Never in cash or to an agent's personal account: it goes to the lawyer's account or to the trust.

Can I buy without the property being public (off-market)?

Yes: there is inventory that isn't published because the owner decided so. We work with an NDA and matching.

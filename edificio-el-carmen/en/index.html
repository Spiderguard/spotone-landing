The building

Commercial ground floor and three levels of units.

Ground floor plus three upper floors (Levels 1–3). Ground floor: two commercial units, about 137 m² each. Levels 1–3: two units per level, 127 to 134 m² enclosed each, with a balcony. Built in 1965, in reinforced concrete. No elevator: main staircase and rear staircase. Covered parking and an interior laundry room.

My take

What you are buying.

It needs a full remodel, and that is already in the price. What you buy is central land with higher-density zoning, and a building with tenants while the project is defined.

It suits a developer looking for land in a central area, someone buying to reposition rents, or a long-term holder or family office.

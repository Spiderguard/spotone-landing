Questions

What people ask me most.

Is it on the beach?

No. It is a country house in Las Fincas, Coronado. Playa Coronado is an 8-minute drive (3 km, per Google Maps); once I send you the exact location, I’ll send the route too.

Is it a single title?

Yes. It is one property of 9,870 m² (about 2.4 acres), registered with Panama’s Public Registry.

How wide and how deep is the land?

According to the lot plan: about 48 m (157 ft) of frontage on the road and 200 to 218 m (656 to 715 ft) deep along the sides. The back boundary is 45 m (148 ft). It is a long, flat lot, with the house in the front half.

How big is the house?

The main house has 238 m² (2,562 sq ft) of enclosed area and 496 m² (5,339 sq ft) under roof. The caretaker’s house adds 50 m² (538 sq ft). These are the measurements from the 2025 appraisal.

How old is it, and what condition is it in?

It was built in 2000. The inside is in good shape; the exterior needs maintenance, so plan on investing something there. The price is in the land.

Is there an HOA or maintenance fee?

No. The owner pays no maintenance fee.

What utilities does it have?

Electricity, phone, municipal water plus a private 150-ft well, and a septic tank. The access road is dirt and gravel, unpaved.

How do I visit?

I group visits on one day and let the caretaker know (the caretaker lives on the property). Tell me which day works for you, whether you’re buying in cash or with financing, and your timeline.

What I do

Four ways to work together.

I work on sales, purchases and rentals of residential and commercial property in Panama. My specialty is Panama City: Costa del Este, Costa Sur, San Francisco, Punta Pacífica and other areas of the city. There I track what competes, and at what price. Outside the city, at the beach, inland in the provinces or anywhere else in the country, I work case by case with the same method.

Let’s talk

Selling or renting out your property

Before a property goes live, I look at what it’s up against: what else is listed nearby, which of those have adjusted their price recently, and how long they’ve been listed. With that comparison I propose the asking price, the angle, the likely buyer and where it gets advertised.

Buying or renting

If you’re buying or renting, I start with what you’re looking for, where and by when. Then I go through the options you are considering, compare them against what else is on the market, contact the broker or owner who has each listing and set up the viewings with the information you need to decide.

Knowing what price the evidence supports

An asking price isn’t always defensible. I review what a property actually competes against and how those prices have moved since they were listed. When I recommend a number, I show what supports it and where I’m still uncertain. It is a commercial recommendation, not an appraisal.

Deciding what to do

If you’re not sure yet whether to sell, rent, buy or wait, I start by laying out where you stand: the area, the price, the timing and the real room to move. Then: what holds up, what needs adjusting and what the next step is.

How I work

How I work with you.

I begin by understanding the decision: what you want to achieve, what could shape the outcome and how much room actually exists.

Context

Objectives, priorities, timing and real budget. Before searching, listing or negotiating, we define what the transaction needs to solve.

Comparison

I review similar listings, their asking prices and time on the market. When verified data on completed sales or rentals is available, I include it.

Strategy

I propose the combination of price, location, presentation, negotiation and timing that makes sense. Every recommendation should be explainable.

Execution

I coordinate the search or marketing process, viewings, negotiation and follow-through to closing.

Questions

What I usually clarify.

If I’m buying, do I have to sign an exclusive agreement to see a property?

No. If another broker has the listing for the property you are interested in, I coordinate with them so you can see it.

What do you do before listing my property?

Before a property goes live, I look at what it’s up against: what else is listed nearby, which of those have adjusted their price recently, and how long they’ve been listed. With that comparison I propose the asking price, the angle, the likely buyer and where it gets advertised.

If I’m the owner, do you ask me for an exclusive agreement?

It is not the first thing I ask for. First I show you what your property competes against and whether other brokers already list it. If exclusivity suits you, we discuss it with that data. Even with exclusivity, I work with other brokers.

Do you work outside Panama City?

Yes. My specialty is Panama City, but I also work beach and inland properties across the country, case by case. Before I recommend anything outside the city, I study the area with the same method: what competes, at what asking price and on what terms. When it helps, I work with a broker who knows the place well. You still deal with one person: me.

If you plan to invest, in Criterio: the maintenance fee · buying to rent and net yield · three numbers before you buy · income or appreciation.

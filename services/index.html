What I do

Four ways to work together.

Owners, buyers, tenants and investors, always with the figure before the adjective.

Let’s talk

The four ways

Taking a property to market

Before a property goes live, I look at what it’s up against: what else is listed nearby, which of those have cut price recently, and how long they've been posted. With that comparison I propose the asking price, the angle, the likely buyer and where it gets advertised.

Buying and renting with representation

If you’re buying or renting, I start with what you’re looking for, where and by when. Then I go through the options you are considering, compare them against what else is on the market, contact the broker or owner who holds it and set up the viewings with the information you need to decide.

Market intelligence

An asking price isn’t always defensible. I review what a property actually competes against and how those prices have moved since they went up. When I recommend a number, I show what supports it and where I’m still uncertain.

Situation assessment

If you’re not sure yet whether to sell, rent, buy or wait, I start by laying out where you stand: the area, the price, the timing and the real room to move. Then what stands up, what needs adjusting and what the next step would be.

How I work

How I work with you.

I begin by understanding the decision: what you want to achieve, what could shape the outcome and how much room actually exists.

Context

Objectives, priorities, timing and real budget. Before searching, listing or negotiating, we define what the transaction needs to solve.

Comparison

I review similar listings, their asking prices and time on the market. When verified data on completed sales or rentals is available, I include it.

Strategy

I propose the combination of price, location, presentation, negotiation and timing that makes sense. Every recommendation should be explainable.

Execution

I coordinate the search or marketing process, viewings, negotiation and follow-through to closing.

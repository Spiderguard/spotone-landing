Services

How I can help.

I work with property owners, buyers, tenants and investors. I also help people relocating to Panama.

When the purchase is an investment, I compare the price against the rent it could achieve, the running costs, vacancy in the area and the resale options. I tell you which figures are verified and which are estimates.

01

Taking a property to market

Before a property goes live, I look at what it's up against: what else is listed nearby, which of those have cut price recently, and how long they've been posted. With that comparison I propose the asking price, the approach, the buyer or tenant profile worth targeting and how the property should be presented.

02

Buying and renting with representation

If you're buying or renting, I start with what you're looking for, where and by when. Then I go through the options you're considering, compare them against what else is on the market, contact the broker or owner who holds each one and arrange the viewings. I stay with you through the negotiation to closing.

03

Market intelligence

An asking price isn't always defensible. I review what a property actually competes against and how those prices have moved since they went up. When I recommend a number, I show what supports it and where the gaps are.

04

Situation assessment

If you're not sure yet whether to sell, rent, buy or wait, I start by laying out where you stand: the area, the price, the timing and the real room to move. Then what stands up, what needs adjusting and what the next step is.

Process

How I work with you.

I begin by understanding the decision: what you want to achieve, what could shape the outcome and how much room actually exists. I then compare the situation with the market, define a strategy and guide its execution.

01

Context

Objectives, priorities, timing and real budget. Before searching, listing or negotiating, we define what the transaction needs to solve.

02

Market comparison

I review similar listings, their asking prices and how long they've been posted. When verified data on completed sales or rentals is available, I include it.

03

Strategy

I propose the combination of price, location, presentation, negotiation and timing that makes sense. Every recommendation should be explainable.

04

Execution

I coordinate the search or marketing process, viewings, negotiation and follow-through to closing.

I explain what I recommend, why and what still needs to be confirmed before proceeding.

Frequently asked

What I usually clarify.

Answers to common questions about working with me.

How does the first conversation work?

The first conversation is for understanding what you are evaluating, what you need and by when. It can begin by WhatsApp, email or a brief call. By the end, we both know whether moving forward makes sense and what the next step would be.

How do I work with you as a buyer?

First, a context conversation to understand what you are looking for and by when. Then I review the options you find, compare them with what else is on offer, coordinate with the broker who holds each property and arrange the viewings. I stay with you through the negotiation to closing.

Do you work with owners who want to sell or rent?

Yes. The process for an owner starts the same way as for a buyer: understanding the property, the market and who it suits. Before listing, I review the price, the timing and how the property is presented.

How does a foreigner buy property in Panama?

In most cases a foreigner can acquire property in Panama. The purchase structure, any restrictions that may apply and the documentation should be reviewed with a lawyer before signing or transferring funds.

Can we start before I move to Panama?

Yes. The process can begin remotely: we compare areas, review prices and evaluate options over WhatsApp or video call. You do not need to be in the country to make decisions with real information.

What if the best decision is not to move forward?

I will tell you. Sometimes the best advice is to wait, adjust the price, change the area or hold for a better moment. That is part of the work too.

Notes for deciding in Panama.

Notes on prices, leases, buildings and neighborhoods. Each answers one specific question.

Market

Higher rates for longer: in Panama, that changes who has the negotiating advantage.

Expensive credit does not stop every transaction. It changes who can move, who has to wait and where the real negotiating room appears.

 Renting

Can a tenant end the lease after an earthquake?

In a residential lease, arguing over whether the earthquake counts as grounds may mean starting with the wrong question.

 Buildings & Neighborhoods

Which structural code was your building designed under?

A building from 1998 and one approved under REP‑2021 did not begin with the same framework. The date helps, but it cannot answer the question alone.

Questions

What I usually clarify.

Tell me your situation and I’ll tell you what I would do.

How does the first conversation work?

It’s to understand what you’re weighing, what you need and by when. It can start on WhatsApp, by email or with a short call. By the end, you know whether to move forward and what comes next.

If I’m an owner, do you ask for exclusivity?

It’s not the first thing I ask for. First I show you what your property is competing with and where its price stands. If exclusivity suits you, we discuss it then. Even with exclusivity, I work with other brokers.

01 — Principle

Not every listed property is properly positioned. Publishing a listing is not the same as going to market. Properties move when price, timing and positioning line up.

I work with people who are willing to make decisions with information — not instinct, not pressure, not haste.

If the read says it is not the right moment yet, I say so. If it says yes, I say that too. That is the work.

Guidance with judgment, not volume.

Four ways of working. All start the same way: understanding what's at stake before deciding.

01

Taking a property to market

Before a property goes live, I look at what it's up against: what else is listed nearby, which of those have cut price recently, and how long they've been posted. With that read I define the asking price, the angle, the likely buyer and where it gets advertised.

02

Buying and renting with representation

If you're buying or renting, I start with what you're looking for, where and by when. Then I go through the options you're considering, compare them against what else is on the market, make the calls and set up the viewings with the information you need to decide.

03

Market intelligence

An asking price isn't always defensible. I review what a property actually competes against and how those prices have moved since they went up. When I recommend a number, I show what supports it and where I'm still uncertain.

04

Situation assessment

If you're not sure yet whether to sell, rent, buy or wait, I start by laying out where you stand: the area, the price, the timing and the real room to move. Then what stands up, what needs adjusting and what the next step would be.

How I work

Read first.
Execute second.

I begin by understanding the decision: what you want to achieve, what could shape the outcome and how much room actually exists. I then compare the situation with the market, define a strategy and guide its execution through closing.

01

Context

Objectives, priorities, timing and real budget. Before searching, listing or negotiating, we define what the transaction needs to solve.

02

Market reading

I compare the situation with active inventory, recent adjustments and signals that may affect the decision. Not to predict, but to reduce uncertainty.

03

Strategy

I define the right combination of price, location, positioning, negotiation and timing. Every recommendation should be explainable.

04

Execution

I coordinate the search or marketing process, viewings, negotiation and follow-through to closing, through one point of contact.

The work is not about showing you more. It is about helping you decide better. If the evidence suggests waiting, adjusting or not moving forward, I will tell you that as well.

What I usually clarify.

The questions that come up most often, answered with the same directness I bring to the work.

How does the first conversation work?

No charge. The first conversation is for understanding what you are evaluating, what you need and by when. It can begin by WhatsApp, email or a brief call. By the end, we both know whether moving forward makes sense and what the next step would be.

How do I work with you as a buyer?
First, a context conversation — no cost — to understand what you are actually looking for. From there, the agreement is clear: one point of contact, in both directions. If you spot a property on any portal, send it to me and I will find it, review it and organize it for you. The commission is paid by the seller at closing — for the buyer, my work has no direct cost.
Do you work with owners who want to sell or rent?
Yes. The process for an owner starts the same way as for a buyer: understanding the property, the market, the ideal buyer profile and the right channel. I do not list for the sake of listing. Before a property moves, there is a read on price, timing and narrative. What goes out well positioned moves.
How does a foreigner buy property in Panama?

In most cases a foreigner can acquire property in Panama. The purchase structure, any restrictions that may apply and the documentation should be reviewed with a lawyer before signing or transferring funds. I walk you through the process and coordinate with your lawyer; this information is for guidance and does not replace legal review.

How do you charge?
Fees depend on the type of transaction and who I represent. Before we begin, I explain in writing how they work in your case and what cost, if any, applies to you.
Can we start before I move to Panama?
Yes. The process can begin remotely: area orientation, market reading and evaluating options over WhatsApp or video call. You do not need to be in the country to make decisions with real information.
What if the best decision is not to move forward?
I will tell you. Sometimes the best advice is to wait, adjust the price, change the area or hold for a better moment. That is part of the work too.

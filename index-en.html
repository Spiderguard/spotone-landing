Questions

What I usually clarify.

Tell me the case, and I tell you what I would do and whether you need a broker.

How does the first conversation work?

The first conversation is for understanding what you are evaluating, what you need and by when. It can begin by WhatsApp, email or a brief call. By the end, you know whether moving forward makes sense and what the next step would be.

How do I work with you as a buyer?

First, a context conversation to understand what you are looking for and by when. Then I review the options you find, compare them with what else is on offer, coordinate with the broker who holds each property and arrange the viewings with the information you need to decide.

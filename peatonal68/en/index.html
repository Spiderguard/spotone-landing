05 · Questions

What I usually clarify.

Does the base rent include maintenance?

No. Units 22 and 23 are $1,800 a month ($18 per m²); maintenance ($250) and waste collection ($15) are paid on top: $2,065 a month before taxes.

Unit 4 is $3,060 a month ($30 per m²) plus maintenance ($255, with waste collection included): $3,315 a month before taxes.

Each listing shows it under See monthly breakdown.

How much is due when I sign the lease?

Signing requires payment of the first month plus one month as a security deposit.

What is not included?

Not included: electricity, internet, permits, build-out, insurance, the business’s own utilities and any other service the tenant contracts directly.

What condition is the unit delivered in?

Unit 22 is shown as an open-plan space with one bathroom. The separation from Unit 21 and the handover condition for both units will be confirmed with management before signing the lease.

Before signing, we put in writing what stays, what is removed and the condition in which the unit is delivered.

What kinds of business can operate here?

Every proposal is reviewed against the tenant mix, the available infrastructure and the approvals it would require.

These are some concepts that could complement the current mix:

  • Skincare and facial aesthetics
  • Spa and massage
  • Aesthetic medicine or dermatology
  • Wellness and athletic recovery
  • Specialized health services
  • Other appointment-based concepts

Tell me what you intend to operate and I will check which units can work before we set up a visit.

Unit 4, on the ground floor, is fitted out for a restaurant and allows a bar, alcohol sales and karaoke.

Do all the units have water, drainage, gas and extraction?

No. Infrastructure varies by unit.

If your business needs water, drainage, gas, extraction or a specific electrical load, that has to be verified before a proposal is put forward.

Can I lease more than one unit?

Units 22 and 23 are adjacent on the second floor and add up to 200 m². Leasing them together is not yet confirmed with management and there is no combined rent set. If you are interested in both, tell me and I will check before offering it.

What are the frontage and depth dimensions?

They vary by unit. Send me the unit number and I will send you the floor plan and the dimensions on file.

How does the parking work?

The plaza has 60 parking spaces: fourteen at street level and forty-six underground, two of them accessible.

Each lease includes one space for the tenant’s use. When it is not in use, it stays available to the plaza’s customers.

How do I arrange a visit?

Send me the unit you are interested in, the type of business and any technical requirement you cannot do without.

First I confirm the space and the infrastructure make sense for your operation; then we set up the visit.

What happens with the furniture in Unit 4's current restaurant?

It is not included in the rent. The current tenant is selling it and it is negotiated directly with them; I can set up that conversation.

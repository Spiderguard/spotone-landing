Why consider it

An operating plaza with complementary uses

The ground floor holds eight restaurants. The upper levels run on appointments, classes and services. For a new tenant that means walking into a plaza that is already operating, with parking available and different reasons to visit throughout the day.

Sixty parking spaces

Fourteen at street level and forty-six covered in the basement, two of them accessible.

Each lease includes one space for the tenant's use. When it is not in use, it stays available to the plaza's customers. For a business that runs on appointments or classes, parking on site answers one of the most common objections to operating in San Francisco.

Eight restaurants on the ground floor

They draw visitors day and night, across eight different kinds of food.

The plaza is located on Calle 68 Este, in an established area of San Francisco surrounded by restaurants, shops and services.

Frequently asked questions
Does the base rent include maintenance and ITBMS?

No. Each listing shows the monthly base rent first and then, separately, maintenance, waste collection and ITBMS. You can also open the breakdown to see the total billed monthly.

How is ITBMS calculated?

ITBMS is Panama's 7% VAT, and it applies to base rent plus maintenance. The $15.00 waste collection fee is exempt, because it is privately contracted collection.

What do I need to sign?

Signing requires payment of the first month plus one month as a security deposit.

What is not included?

Electricity, internet, permits, build-out, insurance, the business's own consumption and any other service the tenant contracts directly are not included.

What condition is the unit delivered in?

It varies by unit. Each listing states the existing bathrooms, air conditioning, partitions, kitchen and lighting.

Before signing, what stays, what is removed and the delivery condition are all put in writing.

What kinds of business can operate here?

Every proposal is reviewed against the tenant mix, the available infrastructure and the approvals it would require.

Tell me what you intend to operate and I will check which units can work before we set up a visit.

Do all the units have water, drainage, gas and extraction?

No. Infrastructure varies by unit.

If your business needs water, drainage, gas, extraction or a specific electrical load, that has to be verified before putting an offer together.

Can I lease more than one unit?

Yes. Units 21 and 22 can form 177 m². Together with Unit 23 they reach 277 m² of continuous space, subject to availability.

What are the frontage and depth dimensions?

They vary by unit. Send me the number and I will send the floor plan and the dimensions on file.

How does the parking work?

The plaza has 60 parking spaces: fourteen at street level and forty-six covered in the basement, two of them accessible.

Each lease includes one space for the tenant's use. When it is not in use, it stays available to the plaza's customers.

How do I arrange a visit?

First I confirm the space and the infrastructure make sense for your operation; then we set up the visit.

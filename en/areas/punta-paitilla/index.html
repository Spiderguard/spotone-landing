The area

The point where the city’s towers began.

Where it is

Punta Paitilla is in the San Francisco district, with Punta Pacífica to the east. It was a US military reservation until 1957, when it returned to Panama.

What you’ll find

Construction began in the late 1960s. Today it is a grid of streets lined with apartment towers. The city lists three neighbourhoods with the name: Paitilla, Nuevo Paitilla and Punta Paitilla.

Getting there

The Corredor Sur ends at the Paitilla viaduct, where Avenida Balboa begins. The first phase of the Cinta Costera runs from Punta Paitilla to Marañón.

What’s nearby

The Cinta Costera waterfront, with parks, courts and a bike path, starts here.

What to check before choosing

At the 2025 workshop, residents pointed to congestion and a lack of parking. The city’s proposal asks new buildings for 10% extra visitor parking and limits retail in several zones. In existing buildings, ask about visitor parking and about nearby projects in the permit stage.

Questions

What people ask me about Punta Paitilla.

Are Paitilla and Punta Paitilla the same?

Not quite. The city lists three neighbourhoods: Paitilla, Nuevo Paitilla and Punta Paitilla, all in San Francisco.

How long have there been towers?

Since the late 1960s, after the area returned to Panama in 1957.

What changes with the new zoning?

The 2025 proposal splits Paitilla into nine zones, limits retail in some and asks new buildings for more visitor parking. In November 2025 a court overturned the change after a challenge by a Paitilla residents’ association; get the current land-use certificate.

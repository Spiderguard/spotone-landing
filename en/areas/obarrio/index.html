The p25–p75 range leaves out the cheapest quarter and the most expensive quarter. These are asking prices, not closing prices.

Days listed is the median number of days since the database first saw each listing, not the portal date, which resets with every repost. The database has full coverage from mid-June 2026. When half or more of the units were already listed by then, the real median falls outside what the database can measure, so all it can say is “N days or more”.

What this data does not say.

  • Days listed is a floor: some sale listings entered the database this week because of coverage gaps, so the real time may be longer.
  • It does not measure closings or absorption. These are asking prices: Encuentra24 records no transactions. Days listed is not time to sell; a high number says the listing is still up, not that it is not selling.
  • Price per m² does not see condition, floor, view or age. It mixes new towers with buildings from the 1970s. Whether something is expensive or cheap is judged against the same floor plan in the same building.
  • Listings repeat. One apartment can carry dozens of listings from different advertisers; here it counts once. The count is an estimate: the real figure sits between units and listings.
  • Listed is not available. The database rarely sees when a listing is taken down.

Method

Active Encuentra24 listings seen in the November 13, 2026 crawl, one unit per repeated combination of price, m² and bedrooms, implausible values removed; cut-off: November 13, 2026.

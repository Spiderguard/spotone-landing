Brand new: 190 m² (2,045 sq ft) on a high floor, with an open view. From the living room and the balcony there is no tower in front: you see the racetrack, the city and, in the distance, the mountains.

The living and dining room opens onto the balcony. The bedrooms sit apart from the social area, each with its own full bath; there is a guest half bath and a separate family den. Marble floors, a kitchen, a laundry room and a maid’s room with its own bath.

Delivered unfurnished. The furnished photos are of the model apartment in the same building and are labeled as such. The model has a decorative ceiling with LED lighting, recessed lights and air conditioners that the unit does not include.

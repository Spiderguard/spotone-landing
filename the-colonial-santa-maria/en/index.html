Questions

What people ask me most.

Is it furnished?

No. It is delivered unfurnished. The furnished photos are of the model apartment in the same building and are labeled as such.

Which photos show the apartment for sale?

The ones labeled “The unit.” The ones labeled “Furnished model” are of the model apartment: they serve as a reference for layout and furnishing. The model has a decorative ceiling with LED lighting, recessed lights and air conditioners that the unit does not include.

What do you see from the balcony?

The racetrack, the city and, in the distance, the mountains. It is a high floor, with no tower directly in front.

What is the layout like?

The social area, with the living and dining room and the balcony, is separate from the bedrooms. All three bedrooms have their own full bath, and there is a guest half bath. There is also a separate family den, a kitchen, a laundry room and a maid’s room with its own bath.

How many parking spaces does it have?

Three.

How much is the maintenance fee?

US$480 a month: US$380 for the building and US$100 for the Santa María fee.

What amenities does the building have?

A pool, a gym, an event room, an air-conditioned kids’ area, a social area with BBQ and 24-hour security.

How do I visit?

I coordinate the visit with the building. Tell me which day works for you, whether you’re buying in cash or with financing, and your timeline.
